## Run ABM like it's your job to hit pipeline, not vanity metrics.

**BlackBelt on Agentic Account Based Marketing (ABM)** is a practitioner's course on ABM for B2B: pick the right target accounts, research them properly, personalize offer and content, build a program, prove it moved revenue — and see where an AI agent can run each step for you. Adapted from a training delivered inside enterprise marketing and sales orgs.

## How this course works

Each module stands on its own — start at Module 1 if you're new to ABM, or jump straight to the process you're stuck on.

1. **Read the module** — Written notes covering the same ground as the original class session.
2. **Pull the slides** — A translated, MegaBrain-branded deck for reference or reuse internally.
3. **Use the template** — ICP scoring sheets, account plans, KPI workbooks — built to fill in, not just read.
4. **Do the exercise** — Every module ends with homework you can run against your own account list.

## Syllabus

Six modules, one per ABM process, in the order you'd actually run them.

### [Module 01 — Fundamentals of ABM](module-1-fundamentals-of-abm/README.md)

A 150-year detour through B2B sales history, why pure inbound breaks down for enterprise accounts, what ABM is, and the three program tiers.

`Beginner` · `Notes + slides` · `~90 min` · `+ 2 agent examples`

### [Module 02 — Selecting Target Accounts](module-2-selecting-target-accounts/README.md)

Build an Ideal Customer Profile, score and rank accounts, and turn a long list of possible customers into a short list worth a dedicated program.

`Beginner` · `Notes + slides + ICP template` · `~60 min` · `+ 2 agent examples`

### [Module 03 — Account Intelligence](module-3-account-intelligence/README.md)

Researching target accounts and stakeholders — four levels of intelligence, where to find it, and how to capture it in an account plan.

`Intermediate` · `Notes + slides + templates` · `~75 min` · `+ 2 agent examples`

### [Module 04 — ABM Metrics](module-4-abm-metrics/README.md)

Three groups of metrics, the waterfall funnel by tier, and how to prove progress before deals close.

`Intermediate` · `Notes + slides + KPI workbook` · `~60 min` · `+ 2 agent examples`

### [Module 05 — Offer & Content Personalization](module-5-personalization/README.md)

Three types of offers and the industry → persona → company layers of personalization, with templates.

`Intermediate` · `Notes + slides + persona kit` · `~75 min` · `+ 2 agent examples`

### [Module 06 — Designing the ABM Program](module-6-program-design/README.md)

Warm-up, engagement, nurture and acceleration — assembling touchpoints into a program, budget, and a RACI-based execution plan.

`Advanced` · `Notes + slides + budget template` · `~90 min` · `+ 2 agent examples`
