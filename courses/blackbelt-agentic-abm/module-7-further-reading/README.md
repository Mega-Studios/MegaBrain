# Further Reading

*Bonus · Not part of the 6-module curriculum*

The original training's "Прочее" ("Miscellaneous") folder held four extra resources the instructors produced alongside the six core modules — an intro e-book, a case-study booklet, and two webinar decks. Unlike the third-party material referenced elsewhere in this course (HBR articles, a NetApp research deck, an outside book summary — none of which are reproduced here), all four of these carry the original training company's own contact footer, so they're the instructors' own work.

They're listed here as bonus reading rather than folded into a module: the content mostly pre-dates and overlaps with what's now in Modules 1-6, and one of the four is written for a very specific 2021 Russian-market moment that doesn't translate to this edition. Full English slide-for-slide decks aren't included in this pass — descriptions below are translated summaries, not full translations.

#### Account-Based Marketing: an introduction (e-book, ~50 pages, 2020)

A standalone primer covering the same ground Module 1 now covers in more depth: why traditional inbound lead-generation breaks down for large, multi-stakeholder b2b deals, the three ABM program tiers, and a quick self-assessment for whether ABM fits your company. Largely superseded by Module 1.

#### 5 ABM Cases: How B2B Leaders Sell Complex Solutions (case booklet, 18 pages)

Five short published case studies, including Autodesk's 10,000 personalized client web portals and an account-based content/community program. Useful as extra worked examples alongside Module 1's OneLogin, Schneider, Trelleborg, and Boon Edam cases.

#### Webinar: The ABM Tech Stack (deck, 159 pages, 2021)

A tour of the ABM tooling landscape circa 2021 — mapped against SiriusDecisions' b2b demand waterfall — including a case study of Acxiom generating $2M of pipeline in 120 days and a walkthrough of intent-data-based audience narrowing. Module 1's "sales tech landscape" and "ABM-programmatic stack" slides cover the same ground with tool names verified current as of July 2026.

#### Webinar: Replacing Western ABM Tools (deck, 170 pages, 2021)

A panel recording with three named industry practitioners discussing alternatives to Western martech after several platforms became unavailable in the Russian market. This one is specific to a 2021 Russian-market disruption and isn't relevant to readers outside that context, so it isn't adapted for this English edition.

> None of the four originals are attached for download here — they're described, not redistributed. If you have access to the original Russian materials and want any of them translated in full for a future edition of this course, that's a reasonable follow-up request.
